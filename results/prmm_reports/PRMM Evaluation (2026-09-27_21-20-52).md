## Methodology
V6 refines PRMM Level 4 scoring with an improvement strength component. It builds on V5 query-comment-based auto-mapping and formula-level caching. Enabled disruptions are the starting scope, detected disruptions determine Level 1 recognition, linked practices build Level 2 coverage, and KPI monitoring builds Level 3. Level 4 now combines (1) coverage: the percentage of L3 KPIs with improvement > tolerance, and (2) strength: the average normalized improvement magnitude (0-100%). The final S4 score blends these: S4_final = 0.5 × S4_coverage + 0.5 × S4_strength, providing a more proportional assessment that considers both how many KPIs improved and how much they improved. Level 5 is not implemented.
